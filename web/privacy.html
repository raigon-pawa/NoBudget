Privacy policy

NoBudget · last updated 19 November 2026

NoBudget is a budgeting app for Android and the web. This page explains what it stores, where, and how to remove it. The short version: your budget stays on your device unless you choose to sign in, and nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used to track you.

Without an account

Everything you enter (accounts, transactions, repeating payments, goals, debts and settings) is saved only on your device: in the app's storage on Android, or in your browser's local storage on the website. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data removes it.

With an account (optional)

Signing in backs your budget up and keeps it in step across your devices. It uses Google Firebase:

  • Sign-in (Firebase Authentication): your email address, and your password stored by Firebase in hashed form. If you continue with Google, Firebase also receives your Google account's name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your budget (Cloud Firestore): a copy of your budget data, stored in Google's Sydney, Australia region and readable only when signed in as you.

Google processes this data on NoBudget's behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

Exchange rates

To convert currencies, the app downloads a public list of daily rates from GitHub, at most twice a day. The request contains nothing about you or your budget.

What NoBudget does not do

  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • NoBudget does not log or store your IP address. As with any internet service, Google (for Firebase) and GitHub (for the website and the rates file) may process it to run and protect their services.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, files or bank accounts.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open the menu and choose Delete account, in the Android app or on the website at nobudget.raigonpawa.dev. This permanently deletes your sign-in and the cloud copy of your budget straight away; the budget on that device stays until you remove the app or clear the site's data. If you can no longer sign in, email nobudget@raigonpawa.dev from the account's address and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

NoBudget is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact
