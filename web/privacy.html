Privacy policy

NoBudget · last updated 19 November 2026

NoBudget is a budgeting app for Android and the web, made by Raigon Pawa (the developer named on Google Play). This page explains what it stores, where, for how long, and how to remove it. Questions: nobudget@raigonpawa.dev.

The short version: your budget is saved on your device, signing in is optional, and nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used to track you. To delete your account, see Delete your NoBudget account and data.

Your budget, on your device

What you enter is financial information: your accounts (names, balances, credit limits and loan amounts), transactions with their categories and notes, repeating payments, goals, budgets and settings. NoBudget saves it on your device: in the app's storage on Android, or in your browser's storage on the website. Without an account, NoBudget never sends it anywhere.

On Android, if your phone's backup is turned on, Android includes the app's data in your own Google account backup and restores it when you reinstall or move to a new phone. That backup is kept by Google under your Google account and your phone's backup settings, not by NoBudget.

With an account (optional)

Signing in keeps a copy of your budget in the cloud so you can use it on your other devices. Two Google Firebase services do this on NoBudget's behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication (runs in the United States) holds your sign-in: your email address, your password stored only as a hash, a user ID, and when the account was created and last signed in. If you continue with Google, it also receives your Google account's name, a link to its profile picture, and its account ID. To prevent abuse it logs the IP addresses and device or browser details used to sign up and sign in.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google's Sydney, Australia region) holds one copy of your budget, the financial information listed above, which only your signed-in account can read or change from the app.

Google processes this data for NoBudget under the Firebase privacy and security terms. If you continue with Google, Google's own privacy policy also applies to your Google account.

Exchange rates and the website

To convert currencies, the app downloads a public list of daily rates from GitHub, at most twice a day. The request contains nothing about you or your budget.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads Firebase's and Google's sign-in scripts and some fonts from Google's servers. As with any website, GitHub and Google see your IP address and browser details when they serve these files.

What NoBudget does not do

  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting, tracking or advertising cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • NoBudget's own code never logs or stores your IP address. The services above see it, as described.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, files or bank accounts.

Security

  • Every connection uses HTTPS, and Google encrypts the Firebase data it stores.
  • Security rules let each account read and change only its own copy, and Firebase stores passwords only as hashes.
  • The cloud copy is not end-to-end encrypted, so the developer could technically open it in the Firebase console. It is only accessed to carry out a request from you, such as deleting an account.

How long data is kept

  • On your devices: until you delete it there (uninstall the app or clear its storage on Android, or clear the site's data in your browser).
  • Your account and its cloud copy: until you delete them. NoBudget keeps no backups of its own.
  • After you delete your account, it and the cloud copy are removed from Firebase straight away. Google removes the sign-in data from its own backups within 180 days and keeps the sign-in IP logs for a few weeks.
  • Deletion request emails are kept only as long as needed to carry them out.

Delete your NoBudget account and data

In the NoBudget Android app, or on the website at nobudget.raigonpawa.dev, while online:

  1. Sign in, if you are signed out.
  2. On the Overview tab, open the ⋯ (More) menu at the top right of the Net worth card.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password, or confirm with Google, and choose Delete account.

Forgot your password? Sign out, choose Forgot password? on the sign-in page to reset it, then follow the steps above, or use email as below.

Deleted straight away: your sign-in (email address, password hash, user ID and any Google name, picture link and account ID) and the cloud copy of your budget. Other devices that were signed in stop syncing and sign out.

Kept: the budget on each of your devices, until you remove it there (plus any Android backup in your Google account), and Google's own backups and sign-in logs for the periods above.

Delete only some data: while signed in, delete those transactions, accounts, goals or repeating payments in the app. The cloud copy is updated without them within seconds.

Can't sign in? Email nobudget@raigonpawa.dev from the account's email address, and the account and its cloud copy will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

NoBudget is meant for adults and is not directed at children.
